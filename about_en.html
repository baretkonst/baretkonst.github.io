Om mig

I hold a Master's degree in Engineering Physics and a PhD in Theoretical Chemistry from Lund University. Following my doctoral studies, I completed my teacher training in Malmö and currently work as a high school teacher in mathematics and physics in Lund.

I have had a passion for drawing and painting since childhood, a creative drive I resumed after my school years. During my time at university, I was introduced to programming, which opened up a completely new realm of creative possibilities.

Today, I bridge my interests in art, mathematics, and programming by crafting digital imagery through code and computational tools.
